Where do these numbers come from?

Real daily prices from Oct 2021 to Oct 2026 for two index funds in £: Vanguard FTSE All-World (shares) and iShares Global Aggregate Bond, £-hedged (bonds), mixed to this pie’s split. A real version would use J.P. Morgan’s own portfolio prices.

No fees in the replay: fees are waived until your pot reaches £1,000. The 5-year history is shown after an example fee of 0.95% a year.

Education, not advice. Results shown after fees, alongside 5-year history. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.
